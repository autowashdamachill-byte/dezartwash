Delete your DezartWash account

DezartWash (Dezart Car Wash), operated by Damac Hills Company, Ibn Al Kahteer, Sulmaniya, Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

You can delete your account and the personal data tied to it at any time. This applies to customers, riders and service stations.

Option 1: delete inside the app

  1. Open the DezartWash app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (or Settings) and tap Delete account.
  3. Verify it is you: with the code we send you, or with Google or Apple if you signed in that way.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, email sales@damachills.sa with the subject "DEZART CAR WASH - Account Deletion Request". Include the name, phone number or email you used in the app so we can find the account. We may ask you to confirm you own it. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, phone, email).
  • Saved addresses and vehicles.
  • Your messages with riders and stations.
  • Identity and business documents uploaded by riders and stations.
  • Your push-notification token.

What may be kept

Booking and payment records that the law or accounting rules require us to keep. We anonymise them so they no longer identify you. Card details are held by our payment provider, Moyasar, under its own rules, not by us.
