Privacy Policy

DezartWash (Dezart Car Wash), operated by Damac Hills Company, Ibn Al Kahteer, Sulmaniya, Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia ("we", "us"). We are the data controller and process personal data under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data the DezartWash mobile app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it.

1. Data we collect

2. How we use it

To create and complete bookings, assign approved riders and stations, take payment, send notifications and verification codes, provide support, prevent fraud and keep the platform safe, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us run the app, under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase - database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
  • Moyasar - card payments (and instalment providers such as Tabby or Tamara, if we offer them).
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Sign-In, Gemini document checks) and Apple (Sign in with Apple).
  • Authentica (SMS verification codes) and Brevo (email verification codes).
  • The rider or service station assigned to your booking sees your name, phone, vehicle, address and booking details.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

4. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account we remove your sign-in, profile, personal messages and uploaded documents. Booking and payment records that we must keep for accounting or legal reasons are retained in anonymised form.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile or Settings, then Delete account) or by following our deletion page.
  • Ask to see or correct your data, or object to its use, by contacting us (section 8).
  • Turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone settings. Some features then stop working.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

7. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact Damac Hills Company, Ibn Al Kahteer, Sulmaniya, Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, by email at sales@damachills.sa (website: sites.google.com/view/dezartcarwash). You may also complain to the competent Saudi data protection authority. We reply within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will notify you in the app of significant changes.
